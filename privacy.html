Train² Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Terms of Use

Train² is a personal workout app for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone, and we collect nothing.

Data we collect

None. Train² has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app never sends any data to us or to any server we operate.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following in its private app storage on your phone, where other apps cannot read it:

  • your exercise library, workouts, weekly plan and workout history;
  • your settings (audio, appearance, calendar);
  • custom calendar pattern images you choose through the Android photo picker. Only the image you pick is copied into the app; the app has no access to your other photos.

You can erase all of it at any time with Settings → Data → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app.

Backups

Android may include the app's data in the device backup to your Google account and in device-to-device transfers when you set up a new phone. This is a system feature under your control (Settings → Google → Backup on most devices); Train² does not read, send or receive that backup itself. Google's handling of backups is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube videos

Exercise demo videos are played through YouTube's embedded player, which runs inside the app using YouTube API Services. When you open a video, YouTube and Google receive the request, including your IP address and standard device information, and may set cookies in the player, exactly as when you watch an embedded video on any website. That processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. Train² does not receive any of that information and does not use the YouTube API to collect, store or share data about you. Videos only load when you tap a video button; nothing is fetched in the background.

Train² is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, used solely to load YouTube videos you ask for. It does not access your location, contacts, microphone, camera, files or health data.

Children

Train² is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 18.
