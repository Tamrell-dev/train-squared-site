Train²

A personal workout app for Android

Train² guides your workouts by sound: spoken cues, tempo pacing and rest timers, so a session runs hands-free once you press start. It builds and schedules workouts from your own exercise library and steps each exercise forward as it gets easier. Everything stays on your phone; there is no account and no data collection.
