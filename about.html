About Train²

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Train² is a personal workout app for Android, built by one developer who wanted a training app that could be run entirely by ear.

What it does

  • Hands-free sessions. Once you press start, spoken cues, a tempo metronome and rest timers walk you through every set, so you never need to look at the phone.
  • Your own library. Build exercises and workouts with sets, reps, weight, tempo and rest, and attach a demo video to each exercise.
  • Progressive overload. Rate each exercise after a session and the app steps it forward when it gets easy, or back when it gets too hard.
  • Scheduling. Plan workouts on a weekly schedule and follow them on a calendar that shows what's done, partial and missed.
  • Built-in programs. A five-phase calisthenics program and a set of mobility routines to get started with.

Private by design

There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Support the app
